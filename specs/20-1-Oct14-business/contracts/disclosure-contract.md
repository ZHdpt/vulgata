## Decision and tradeoffs

Use **self-contained, owner-protected opaque dependency capsules**, issued with an exact successful page. Do not expose a contributor descriptor in page JSON, infer a union from citations, or add a per-read dependency table/service. The host's existing authenticated Data Protection provider supplies encryption/integrity under a distinct disclosure purpose. A capsule does not grant permission and has no expiry that substitutes for current authorization.

This keeps graph authorization independent of dialogue persistence and supports retained history without an additional read-store lookup. Costs are bounded host-only payloads and a real key-retention obligation. Decryption failure after key loss, rotation without old keys, a foreign purpose or version mismatch fails closed; it never falls back to footers, selected Repository IDs or current Document versions. Durable dialogue stores the capsule, not merely its Guid. Host key retention and safe protection-provider composition are integration prerequisites; this checkpoint changes no key ring or global configuration.

`KnowledgeDependencyHandle(SetId, BindingDigest, ProtectedPayload)` and `KnowledgeConsumedContentHandle(ReceiptId, BindingDigest, ProtectedPayload)` are host-only transports. All visible identity/digest fields must match the authenticated protected payload. Constructors confer no issuer authority. Stable semantic digests exclude randomized issuance IDs, ciphertext, issuance time and ephemeral authentication stamps/tokens; actual authenticated issuance IDs remain integrity-bound inside the capsule. Repeated ciphertext is not the idempotency comparison. Retried operations retain their original agreed semantic input and cannot substitute a newer history/read selection.

## Completeness and exact result binding

The owner constructs a successful `KnowledgeDisclosedPage<T>` during the same bounded read that selects and materializes its page. It binds the canonical page digest, exact consumer-output digest/profile, fixed selection and complete owner-known origin groups before final live checks/logging. A successful result is one atomic host object: page, capsule and exact encoded output. No later fetch-dependencies operation may be paired with a different page.

An origin group retains exact immutable owner provenance and its complete transitive contributor union, including source and advisory contributors that are not quoted. The owner includes contributors controlling:

- successful search/browse titles, excerpts, qualifications, provenance, coverage and continuation/exhaustion metadata;
- exact content, all supplied claims/evidence/limitations, section/chunk identity and source coverage;
- related endpoint identities, directed accepted meaning, relation-only evidence contributors, permitted Uncertainties and coverage;
- every permitted fact influencing exposed labels, counts or page metadata, even when no target is subsequently read or cited.

A conservative authorized superset is permitted; a subset is not. Owner-side complete contributor records remain authoritative. Public `EvidenceReference`, public graph nodes or citation footers cannot establish completeness. Denied/hidden records are filtered before metadata decisions, and may not be revealed indirectly by adding an origin or cursor. Failed, cancelled, refused or oversized reads yield no successful page/capsule/input or content-read credit.

Each capsule binds subject ID, complete `PublicationBinding`, mode, purpose and canonical Repository scope. Fresh calls supply a new host-derived `KnowledgeSubject` and recheck current account/stamp/role/contributors against the same retained data selection. Authentication stamps and expiring `ReadSelection.Token` are not durable permission fields. Scope, purpose or mode changes cannot transplant an old union; Business cannot reuse IT capsules and Graph cannot relabel a Chat capsule. A reopened selection for the same binding may reauthorize retained capsules after the old cursor expires. Every Document-bearing origin retains its exact kind; a null kind is allowed only for an explicit no-Knowledge root or safe non-Document coverage metadata and cannot bypass Business BL-only enforcement. Reauthorization rejects a Business capsule containing any CL-disclosing origin, regardless of a caller's nullable-kind choice.

## Initialization, history and accumulated state

The trusted Chat coordinator owns completeness of the **actual input ledger**. Knowledge can authenticate the pages it issued; it cannot discover unregistered inputs by examining an empty caller list. No generic host-input-proof framework is added.

`CreateNoKnowledgeRootAsync` issues an explicit root only after current owner/selection checks for an exact initial projection consisting of the user's question and static instructions, or an empty initial graph. The supplied initial-input digest binds that projection. This operation is not available as an explanation for missing provenance: existing generated prose, historical turns, advisory facts or supplied Knowledge context prohibit claiming a no-Knowledge root for that context. Additional advisory facts must enter through an owner read with a capsule. Unknown provenance stops input/disclosure.

History retrieval by the dialogue store supplies the saved completed turns' complete inference capsules, including recursively inherited uncited input origins. It reauthorizes and merges those capsules with the current initial root before reusing history. An empty footer does not erase history lineage. Flatten groups on union; never nest growing ciphertext or substitute only the immediately preceding turn's references. The coordinator retains the union of all earlier admitted input groups and adds a new page before model submission. Dependencies never disappear to fit a limit.

`MergeAsync` authenticates and merges compatible capsules under live checks. `ReauthorizeAsync` can check a whole bounded batch directly without first creating a merged capsule. Empty/default/missing arrays are `UnknownLineage`, not an authorized empty union. A valid issued no-Knowledge root is distinct from absent proof, an invalid/forged proof, denial and authority outage. Later Knowledge origins change the aggregate from no-Knowledge to Knowledge-bearing without dropping its root identity.

Chat rechecks the accumulated union before provider submission, released text, accepted report/footer, terminal save, display and history reuse. A denial/outage suppresses the whole dependent new material and stops work; removing only a reference footer cannot authorize prose. Previously transmitted bytes cannot be recalled. Saved receipt truth and current permission to disclose its text remain separate.

## Exact consumed-content proof

The content-read ledger and the inference union answer different questions. The union covers every inference input. A reference's consumed-content proof establishes which exact content stream was successfully admitted; it does not establish total source-analysis coverage.

`BuildConsumedContentAsync(selection, document, admittedContentPages)` authenticates owner content-page origins for that exact batch/publication/logical/version/section target. Owner origins contain the stable stream identity and complete derived-projection digest, exact start/end positions, the raw UTF-8 chunk digest, whether the stream ended, raw content UTF-8 byte count and qualified source coverage. Establish chain adjacency and replay identity from these owner-validated semantic positions/digests, never equality of encrypted continuation strings: Data Protection can produce a different `Next` token for the same returned position. Identical semantic chunk replay adds no credit even if its wire token differs. The same position with changed chunk payload, projection or owner coverage is a conflict (`InvalidProof`), not another successful chunk. Missing initial, middle or final intervals remain partial. Whole Document and individual sections are distinct streams. Search/related responses and refused/oversized results never fill a content interval. The coordinator registers only results that passed actual encoding/accounting and were admitted as input; a conservative inference union may include more than the reference ledger.

The result is an owner-protected `KnowledgeConsumedContentHandle` and resolved `KnowledgeConsumedContentReceipt` with exact Document, partial flag, successful unique chunk count, raw `ContentBytes`, source coverage and content-origin dependency capsule. Source coverage null means unknown; a complete stream can still describe partial source analysis. A partial stream remains visibly partial.

`VerifyConsumedContentAsync(selection, proof, inferenceDependencies)` authenticates the proof, resolves its facts, checks current authority and verifies its content-page origins are included in the full inference capsule. Dialogue compares the reported reference with these facts. Its consumed-target DTO stores the reference plus proof handle; it does not duplicate the full inference capsule or accept caller-supplied coverage/counts as authority. A consumed proof contains only its own bounded content-origin/position data, never nested full inference ciphertext. The save request supplies the full inference capsule once.

## Real consumer byte compatibility

`IOctoberKnowledgeOutputEncoder` is a trusted, versioned, mechanical host adapter, not a model-provided callback or an authorization port. It encodes the complete actual consumer result wrapper with the consumer's actual serializer. It is deterministic, side-effect-free and bounded, and may not add unrelated content or strip/alter the owner page. `ProfileId` identifies the fixed wrapper/serializer version whose conformance is reviewed.

`KnowledgeReadOutputBudget` carries the host's current per-result limit and remaining aggregate allowance. Both can narrow existing owner limits and never reset a turn's budget. The owner builds a candidate satisfying its existing bare-page bounds, invokes the typed encoder, and measures the **actual returned UTF-8 bytes**. It selects a smaller safe content chunk or page and its genuine continuation as needed. No arbitrary `64 KiB minus N`, consumer truncation, invented cursor, qualifier stripping or read credit before successful accounting is allowed. Unsplittable oversized metadata fails safely.

A successful host envelope contains those exact immutable `OutputUtf8` bytes and their SHA-256 digest, plus the canonical page digest. The capsule binds both digests and the encoding profile. The consumer explicitly submits that JSON representation without an additional string-quoting layer; it does not reserialize a differently shaped wrapper later. The actual provider/tool adapter must prove its JsonElement path preserves the agreed representation/budget. Returning some different smaller serializer's length is not conformance.

For Chat content, the complete `OctoberChatToolResult(Succeeded, Content: page, References: [])` with its real camelCase/string-enum/null/escaping/MaxDepth profile must fit `min(admitted.ContentBytes, 65,536)`; aggregate actual tool output remains at most the admitted allowance, normally 262,144 bytes. Search, related and other Chat tool results also consume that aggregate. The coordinator accounts all actual outputs, including non-Knowledge/refused outputs, before accepting another input. Explorer's independent 64-KiB page guarantee remains in force and does not prove Chat compatibility.

All `KnowledgeDisclosedPage<T>` properties and the containing result's `Disclosure` property carry `JsonIgnore`. Ordinary System.Text.Json serialization therefore cannot accidentally publish the host envelope or its capsule. Only the deliberate consumer adapter accesses `OutputUtf8`; dependency handles never become model/API/UI fields. Durable host persistence explicitly serializes its capsule fields. This is not permission to reflect over the host envelope in a public endpoint.

## Capsule bounds and canonical identity

The counterproposal to Chat's two-MiB union is **at most 512 KiB for the complete canonical unprotected capsule plaintext**, including its headers/identity/digest, and **at most 1 MiB for actual protected UTF-8 payload**. Both actual byte limits and at most 4,096 distinct contributors / 128 origin groups apply. The same per-proof bounds apply to consumed-content capsules. Protect first only after the canonical bound passes, then check the actual protected bound; neither is estimated with an assumed encryption overhead. Reject invalid Unicode, malformed versions or overflows. Do not drop an old origin to fit.

These limits do not promise every independently maximal field fits every envelope. Dialogue's one-MiB begin and two-MiB terminal limits measure the actual complete request, including every capsule/proof. Reject an oversized dependency extension before sending its input to the model. Host encoding/storage caps are separate from the public 64-KiB projection cap. A configuration or accumulated input that cannot later satisfy required durable bounds is unavailable for continued generation, not a reason to clip lineage.

Canonical semantic encoding uses the existing `OctoberKnowledgePolicy.CanonicalizeJson`: exact text, strict UTF-8, ordinal object keys, Guid D strings, integer enum values and no Unicode normalization. Set-valued scope/contributors/origins are deduplicated then sorted by their canonical encoding; narrative and exact stream interval order remain ordered. Bindings use the complete existing Knowledge/Publication fields and stable subject ID. Per-origin semantic data includes origin kind, exact selection, nullable DocumentKind, complete contributors, exact page/input digest, output profile/digest where applicable, and immutable content-stream/coverage facts where applicable. Stable root-input digests remain present after union. Runtime tokens, authentication stamps, issue times, issuance Guid and ciphertext are excluded from semantic identity.

Dependency `BindingDigest` is uppercase SHA-256 over strict UTF-8 of `october.knowledge.dependencies.v1`, LF, and that canonical semantic object. Consumed-content `BindingDigest` uses `october.knowledge.consumed-content.v1`, LF, and its canonical target/interval/coverage/origin object. The authenticated plaintext additionally carries version, purpose-kind, issuance Guid and the claimed BindingDigest; the owner recomputes and compares the semantic digest after unprotecting. OutputDigest deliberately hashes the exact output bytes, including any newly protected continuation bytes. PageDigest also binds that actual returned page. These exact-delivery bindings may differ on a newly encoded replay; they are not the stable consumed-stream identity used for deduplication. Excluding the dependency capsule's own randomized issuance/seal fields from its semantic identity does not remove exact wire-result binding. PageDigest uses `october.knowledge.page.v1`, LF, the exact page type name, LF, and canonical camelCase JSON of the unchanged page with integer enum values and explicit nulls. The owner publishes concrete codec golden vectors with separately authorized implementation/conformance; this declaration-only checkpoint does not claim executable codec evidence.

Protected purposes must distinguish dependency capsules and consumed-content capsules from existing expiring selection/cursor tokens. A producer cannot present one kind as another. Persistence stores full opaque payloads with their semantic identities, while idempotency encoding uses validated semantic BindingDigest rather than randomized ciphertext/issuance Guid. Owner private payload schema/version is not a consumer contributor DTO: only the owner decodes and validates it.

## Explorer aggregate and accepted edge identity

The accepted graph slice is **one hop, no interactive expansion**, at most 40 nodes and 80 accepted directed edges. Its total is at most eight owner calls and ten seconds, including final authorization. Count selection opening in the same total: one open, at most six acquisition calls, and one final `ReauthorizeAsync` over all acquired dependency capsules under the same caller cancellation/deadline. No extra root/merge call is required for graph acquisition. A final aggregate check requires no separate merge call and no dialogue table. Failed/timed-out/denied final authorization returns no partially trusted graph.

The aggregate includes every successful acquired page influencing nodes, edges, exact identities, meanings, labels, qualified excerpts, Uncertainties, coverage, cursor-derived incomplete flags or counts, including earlier acquisitions within that one-hop construction. Retain a successful page's dependencies even if its display items are later truncated by the graph node/edge budget. Relation-only contributors must remain even when endpoints are otherwise permitted. Do not guess a cited subset and reread it as an authorization surrogate. A future expansion feature would have to keep previous displayed material in its union; this checkpoint does not authorize interactive expansion.

**Additive declaration, pending final candidate compatibility:** after preliminary consumer agreement, root added non-positional `Guid? RelationshipVersionId { get; init; }` to canonical `RelatedDocument`, preserving its existing Kind/From/To/Meaning constructor and deconstruction. The public related-edge JSON can now contain this additional nullable property. A new graph acquisition requires the exact nonempty owner ID; null legacy items cannot become accepted graph edges by deriving an ID from endpoints or meaning. Future reader production populates it from selected immutable relationship records; controlled fixtures must use their accepted fixture IDs. Root owns that existing-record edit. Final consumer review of the exact committed candidate remains required before dependent behavior. Both public page and complete consumer encoding must remeasure any additive property; old size evidence cannot be silently reused.

## Checkpoint verification and stops

Before dependent behavior, consumers review one separate declaration commit and acknowledge the signatures, capsule limits, actual JSON profile, proof ownership and additive edge-ID declaration. Then separately authorized implementation must test forged/foreign/wrong-kind capsules, changed binding, lost keys, retained history after cursor expiry, uncited/advisory/transitive/relation-only/coverage revocation, empty-root versus missing lineage, capacity exhaustion, content gaps/replays, semantic digest vectors and cancellation.

Actual Business-to-Chat tests must encode real near-limit pages through the real wrapper for ASCII, Chinese, supplementary Unicode, quotes, slashes/control escapes, large title/evidence/limitations/cursors and section continuation, checking full-result and aggregate counts. Explorer tests must exercise actual one-hop acquisition and final batch reauthorization within eight calls/ten seconds, with denied/outage results exposing no graph.

Stop now after declaration/document review. Do not implement issuers, reader changes, dialogue storage, providers, migrations, endpoints, graph behavior or runtime in this checkpoint. Missing accepted consumer contract, protection/key retention, complete owner origins, a conformant encoder or current authority blocks the affected later behavior. No new tool, source access, fine-grained ACL, Scan strategy or competing schema is introduced by these declarations.
